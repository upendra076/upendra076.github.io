Privacy Policy

Brick Toe - Offline Puzzles · Last updated: 22 October 2026

This policy explains what information the Brick Toe app ("the app", "we", "us") collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. Brick Toe is published on Google Play by Eleventa.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress, levels, best scores and current boards are saved only on your own device. We do not send them to any server, and we cannot see them. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

The app has no accounts and does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, location or any other personal details.

Age question

Brick Toe is made for players of all ages, including children. The first time the app opens, it asks for the year you were born. The answer is stored only on your device and is used only to choose which kind of ads the app may show and whether the measurement described below is switched on. It is not sent to us or to anyone else.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob.

  • Players under 13: every ad request is marked as directed to children. Ads are not personalised, are limited to content suitable for all ages, and are served under Google's policies for children's apps.
  • Players 13 and over: to show and measure ads, AdMob and its advertising partners may collect information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device type, operating system and how you interact with ads, and may use it to show ads that are more relevant to you.

You can learn how Google uses this information at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Measuring our own advertising (Meta)

For players aged 13 and over only, we use the Meta (Facebook) SDK to understand whether our own ads on Facebook and Instagram lead people to install and open the app. It is never switched on for players under 13. When it is on, the SDK may collect your device's advertising ID and basic app events, such as the app being installed or opened, a game being finished, a level being reached, or a rewarded ad being watched. These events do not include your name or contact details. Meta's data policy is available at facebook.com/privacy/policy.

Your choices

  • You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads on some phones).
  • You can delete all game data stored by the app by uninstalling it or clearing its storage in your phone's app settings.

Children

Brick Toe can be played by children. For players who indicate they are under 13, we do not collect personal information: there are no accounts, the Meta SDK is not used, and ads are child-directed, non-personalised and rated for all ages, as described above. Game progress stays on the device. If you are a parent or guardian and have questions, contact us at the address below.

Security

Information collected by our advertising partners is handled under their own security and privacy practices, described in the policies linked above.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes. The date at the top shows when it was last updated.
