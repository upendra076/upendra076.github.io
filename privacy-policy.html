Privacy Policy

Brick Toe - Offline Puzzles · Last updated: 21 October 2026

This policy explains what information the Brick Toe app ("the app", "we", "us") collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. Brick Toe is published on Google Play by Eleventa.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress, levels, best scores and current boards are saved only on your own device. We do not send them to any server, and we cannot see them. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

The app has no accounts and does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, location or any other personal details.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, AdMob and its advertising partners (which may include Meta Audience Network) may collect information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device type, operating system, and how you interact with ads. This information may be used to show ads that are more relevant to you.

You can learn how Google uses this information at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Measuring our own advertising (Meta)

We use the Meta (Facebook) SDK to understand whether our own ads on Facebook and Instagram lead people to install and open the app. The SDK may collect your device's advertising ID and basic app events, such as the app being installed or opened, a game being finished, a level being reached, or a rewarded ad being watched. These events do not include your name or contact details. Meta's data policy is available at facebook.com/privacy/policy.

Your choices

  • You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads on some phones).
  • You can delete all game data stored by the app by uninstalling it or clearing its storage in your phone's app settings.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help address it.

Security

Information collected by our advertising partners is handled under their own security and privacy practices, described in the policies linked above.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes. The date at the top shows when it was last updated.
